// Verification Pipeline

How TruthLens Works

A transparent 4-stage pipeline combining NLP syntactic analysis with authoritative multi-source evidence grounding.

1
SUBMIT

Paste a news article, headline, specific factual claim, or live URL for extraction and validation.

2
ANALYZE

AI extracts discrete factual claims, evaluates sentiment, subjectivity, sensationalism, and clickbait patterns.

3
VERIFY

The system cross-references extracted claims against reliable scientific, wire, and institutional knowledge bases.

4
RESULT

Receive definitive classification (Real, Fake, Misleading, Unverified), calibrated confidence, and full explanation.

// About The Project

What Is Fake News Detection?

Our system uses natural language processing, machine learning, and evidence analysis to help users evaluate online information objectively. Rather than acting as a black-box oracle, TruthLens breaks down claims, identifies linguistic manipulation, and cites public evidence.
