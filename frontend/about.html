// What Is Fake News Detection?

Combating Misinformation with Interpretable AI

Our system uses NLP, machine learning, and evidence analysis to help users evaluate online information. Rather than presenting subjective opinions, TruthLens identifies claims, analyzes content patterns, compares available evidence, and presents the reasoning behind every result.

Open Source Models
Fine-tuned transformers and TF-IDF classifiers trained on verified fact-checking datasets.
Transparent Citing
Every contradicted or supported verdict references primary public registries and official sources.
