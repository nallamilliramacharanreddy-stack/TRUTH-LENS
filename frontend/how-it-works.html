The 4-Step Verification Workflow

1
SUBMIT

Paste a news article, URL, headline or claim. URL submissions are parsed for title, publisher, author, date, and body paragraphs.

2
ANALYZE

AI extracts discrete claims, entities, sentiment, subjectivity, sensationalism, and clickbait patterns using fine-tuned BERT and lexical models.

3
VERIFY

The system searches verified databases, official gazettes, and peer-reviewed journals to find corroborating or contradicting primary records.

4
RESULT

Receive classification (REAL, FAKE, MISLEADING, UNVERIFIED), calibrated confidence score, and bullet-point explanations.

// Explainable AI (XAI)

Distinguishing Model Prediction from Factual Verification

A core innovation of TruthLens is its strict avoidance of presenting pure AI probability as undeniable truth. Machine learning models can detect stylistic bias, but true factuality requires grounding in verifiable external evidence.

AI Prediction: Statistical probability derived from training corpus word distributions and stylometric patterns.
Factual Evidence: Primary documentation retrieved from indexed health authorities, space agencies, and wire services.
Grounded Verdict: Weighted fusion producing transparent confidence calibration and auditable reason codes.

Text Preprocessing & Linguistic Signals

1. Preprocessing Sequence

Raw Input → HTML Tag Stripping → Text Normalization → Sentence Splitting → Word Tokenization → Stopword Removal → Named Entity Extraction.

2. Claim Extraction Engine

Deconstructs sentences into Subject-Predicate-Object triplets, date references, and geographic anchors to query structured fact databases.

3. Linguistic Risk Signals

Measures emotional fear triggers, clickbait phrasing ("you won't believe"), urgent manipulation cues ("share before deleted"), and subjective bias ratios.
